How the spaced-repetition schedule works

Reveal the answer, then rate your recall. Again repeats the card in about 10 minutes. Hard, Good, and Easy schedule increasingly longer intervals. Successful reviews expand the interval using an SM-2-inspired ease factor; missed cards shorten it. Keyboard shortcuts: 1–4 rate a revealed card, Space flips, arrow keys navigate, and P plays pronunciation.

Progress is stored only in this browser. Clearing browser data or using another device starts a separate schedule.
